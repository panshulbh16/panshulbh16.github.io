How the solver calculates
  • Ending on hand = Beginning on hand + Firm supply + Planned receipts − Total demand. Each period starts with the prior period's ending on hand; period 1 starts with on hand today.
  • Total demand uses sales orders only inside the demand time fence. Outside it, the consumption rule applies. Shrinkage then grosses it up: demand ÷ (1 − shrinkage).
  • When projected inventory drops below safety stock (or below the min for min–max), the solver creates a planned receipt. Size comes from the lot sizing rule, then minimum order qty, then fixed lot multiple, then rounding. Maximum order qty splits the quantity into several orders.
  • No planned orders are created inside the planning time fence; shortages there become expedite and reschedule-in messages.
  • Planned order release = due date − total lead time, shown as start quantity (receipt ÷ yield). Releases falling before the horizon start are past due. Releases inside the release time fence are flagged to release now.
  • Safety stock by days of cover uses average daily demand over the current and next 3 periods. Statistical uses z(service level) × σ of period demand × √(lead time ÷ period length).
  • With capacity on, firm + planned supply can't exceed capacity in a period. Build ahead moves the shortfall into the latest earlier periods with spare capacity, outside the planning time fence, in lot-size multiples where capacity allows.
  • Days of cover = ending on hand ÷ average daily demand of the next 4 periods. Weekly and monthly plans keep separate inputs; parameters in days convert to each bucket size. Entries are saved in this browser only.
