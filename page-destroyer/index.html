Page Destroyer

Smash any webpage. Feel better.

A slow portal, a ticket that hasn’t moved in weeks, a form that just lost your work. Click the icon and take it apart with a hammer, a gun, a chainsaw or fire. Repair it with one key when you’re done.

Four weapons, one key each

Pick from the glass toolbar at the bottom of the screen or press the number. Every hit adds to a running damage bill.

  • 🔨1

    Hammer

    Click to shatter the screen. Cracks spread out from the hit and glass shards fly off.

  • 🔫2

    Gun

    Hold the mouse down to keep firing. Bullet holes, sparks and spent shells bouncing off the bottom edge.

  • 🪚3

    Chainsaw

    Drag to cut a jagged line through anything. The engine revs faster the faster you move.

  • 🔥4

    Fire

    Hold and sweep to burn the page. Flames rise from the cursor and leave scorch marks behind.

  • C Repair the page
  • M Sound on or off
  • Esc Exit
  • Alt Shift D Start on any page

Install

Until the Chrome Web Store listing is live, load it directly. It works in Chrome, Edge, Brave and other Chromium browsers.

  1. Download page-destroyer.zip and unzip it. You get a folder called page-destroyer.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and switch on Developer mode in the top right.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the page-destroyer folder.
  4. Pin it from the puzzle-piece menu, then click it on any page or press Alt Shift D.

Add it from the Chrome Web Store, pin it from the puzzle-piece menu, then click it on any page or press Alt Shift D.

Add to Chrome

Chrome doesn’t let extensions run on its own pages, so it won’t start on chrome:// pages or the Chrome Web Store.

Privacy

Page Destroyer collects nothing.

It runs only on the tab where you click it, draws on top of the page, and forgets everything when you exit. No accounts, no tracking, no network requests. Read the privacy policy.
