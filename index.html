About

I’m a product engineer: I take an idea to something people use, then keep it working honestly in production. At work that means enterprise CRM on Oracle Fusion Cloud; on my own time it means full-stack products, from the database schema to the copy on the buttons.

The work I publish under my own name is product engineering, not a gallery of unfinished experiments. The four live products above are full-stack TypeScript apps I designed and implemented, and Molbhav is a working demo you can click through.

I like the intersection of product and platform: a score you can explain, a daily quota that cannot be bypassed in the client, a payment webhook that still grants access if the tab closes. That is the standard I hold my own shipping to.
